INDUSTRIAL & COMMERCIAL WASTEWATER REUSE PROTOCOL (2024)
Section 22.1: Chemical Discharge Limits
Small-scale industrial units (including commercial laundries and car washes) are strictly prohibited from discharging untreated effluent containing phosphates or high-alkali detergents into the municipal sewerage system. All units must install a 'Primary Grease-and-Silt Trap' and verify that pH levels of discharge remain between 6.5 and 8.5. 

Section 24.5: On-Site Water Recycling Incentives
Commercial establishments that implement a Closed-Loop Recycling System (reusing at least 60% of process water) are eligible for a 15% 'Green Credit' on their monthly utility bill. Verification requires a bi-annual audit by a certified Municipal Environmental Inspector.

Section 26.2: Illegal Sullage Dumping
The dumping of 'sullage' (wastewater from sinks and showers) into open storm-water drains is a punishable offense. In residential zones, sullage must be diverted to an approved greywater treatment unit or the centralized sewer line. Violations carry a mandatory fine of ₹2,500 per incident for first-time offenders.
